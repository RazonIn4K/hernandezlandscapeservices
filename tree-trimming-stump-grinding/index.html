Frequently Asked Questions

How often should trees be trimmed?

Most residential trees benefit from periodic pruning to remove dead wood, improve clearance from roofs and walkways, and reduce storm risk. We recommend an on-site look before scheduling.

Will you grind the stump after trimming or removal?

Yes. Stump grinding can be added to a trimming or removal job so the stump is taken below grade and the area is ready for seed or landscaping.

Can you work in tight yards or limited driveway access?

Yes. We plan equipment and drop zones around access constraints and leave the work area cleaned up when hauling is included.
