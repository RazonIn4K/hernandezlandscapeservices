Frequently Asked Questions

When should I schedule leaf removal?

Most leaf cleanups are scheduled in the fall, and spring cleanups are available for leaves and debris left over the winter.

Do you haul the leaves away?

Yes. Hauling can be included in the cleanup estimate, so leaves and yard debris leave with the crew.

Can leaf removal be combined with other services?

Yes. Leaf removal is often combined with fall cleanup, gutter cleaning, and final mowing visits in one estimate.
